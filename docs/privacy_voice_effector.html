Voice Effector Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2018

Voice Effector ("us", "we", or "our") operates the Voice Effector mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

We use your data to provide and improve the Service. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information Collection And Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

We do not ask you to provide any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, or phone number, and we do not operate any servers that store your data.

Voice Recordings

The Service uses your device's microphone, with your permission, only while you are recording your voice. Your recordings, the audio files you save, your effect settings, and your purchase status are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to third parties.

Audio files are shared with other apps or services only when you choose to share them using the share feature.

Usage Data

When you use the Service, our Service Providers may automatically collect certain information ("Usage Data").

This Usage Data may include information such as the type of mobile device you use, your mobile operating system, the IP address of your mobile device, an app instance identifier, how you interact with the Service, crash logs and other diagnostic data.

Tracking & Advertising Identifier

We use the advertising identifier of your device (IDFA) to show advertisements that match your interests only if you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency. You can use all features of the Service even if you do not allow tracking.

You can change this permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.

Use of Data

Voice Effector uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To show advertisements so that we can keep providing the Service for free
  • To verify and restore your in-app purchases
  • To provide customer care and support
  • To provide analysis or valuable information so that we can improve the Service
  • To monitor the usage of the Service
  • To detect, prevent and address technical issues

Transfer Of Data

Your information may be transferred to — and maintained on — computers located outside of your state, province, country or other governmental jurisdiction where the data protection laws may differ than those from your jurisdiction.

Our Service Providers may process your information in the United States and other countries.

Voice Effector will take all steps reasonably necessary to ensure that your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy and no transfer of your Personal Data will take place to an organization or a country unless there are adequate controls in place including the security of your data and other personal information.

Disclosure Of Data

Legal Requirements

Voice Effector may disclose your Personal Data in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to:

  • To comply with a legal obligation
  • To protect and defend the rights or property of Voice Effector
  • To prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the Service
  • To protect the personal safety of users of the Service or the public
  • To protect against legal liability

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Retention And Deletion Of Data

Data stored on your device, including your recordings, saved audio files and effect settings, is kept until you delete it in the Service or delete the Service from your device.

Data collected by our Service Providers is kept in accordance with the retention policies of each Service Provider.

Your Choices

  • Tracking. You can withdraw your permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.
  • Ad Consent. If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you can change or withdraw your consent at any time from "Privacy settings" in the settings screen of the Service.
  • Microphone. You can withdraw your permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone on your device.
  • Deletion. Deleting the Service from your device removes all data stored on your device. To request deletion of data collected by our Service Providers, please contact us. We will ask our Service Providers to delete it to the extent we are able to.
